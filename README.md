## The story
It's the year 2500, the player is 20 years old. You cannot choose which character class to play, as it is randomly assigned to you.
You start as a "worker" or a "miner", in rare cases also as a "scientist".
You start in a Mars colony and you have to do stupid work until you eventually are able to buy a spaceship. 
You can then explore our solar system and earn more money, get more experience, upgrade your skills...

As you are getting older, you want to have a partner, marry, get children.

Because when you are too old and have no children, the game is over.
